# User Manual

## Getting Started

1. **Register** an account from the homepage (name, email, password).
2. **Log in** with your credentials.
3. You'll land on your **Dashboard**, showing your detection stats.

## Running a Detection

1. Open **Image Recognition** from the sidebar.
2. (Optional) Expand **Detection Settings** to choose a model size
   (Nano is fastest, Large is most accurate) and confidence threshold.
3. Upload a JPG, PNG, BMP, or WEBP image (up to 15 MB).
4. Review the preview (resolution, file size, format).
5. Click **Recognize Image**.
6. Review the annotated image, the detected-objects table, and summary
   metrics (object count, processing time, confidence range).
7. Download the annotated image, a CSV report, or a JSON report.

## Reviewing History

Open **History** to see every past detection. You can:
- Search by filename or detected object name
- Sort newest/oldest first
- Download a prior annotated image
- Delete a single record, or clear all history

## Analytics

Open **Analytics** for charts on your detection activity: top detected
objects, category breakdown, daily/monthly activity, confidence
distribution, and processing-time trend. Charts populate once you have
a few detections.

## Settings

- **Profile** — update your name and profile picture.
- **Password** — change your password (requires your current one).
- **Preferences** — set your default model, confidence threshold, and
  export format.
- **Reports** — export your full detection history as CSV, Excel, or
  PDF.
- **Notifications** — review system notifications.
- **About** — version and system info.

## Logging Out

Click **Logout** in the sidebar at any time.
